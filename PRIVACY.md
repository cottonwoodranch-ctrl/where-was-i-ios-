Where Was I: Daily Recall collects no personal data. The app does not have an account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Microphone audio stays on your iPhone, in memory only, and is never saved to a file, uploaded, or shared with the developer or any third party.

## Audio

The app uses the microphone only while it is open on the screen and listening is not paused. Leaving the app, or tapping **Pause listening**, stops capture.

Audio is converted on the device to 16 kHz mono samples and kept in a rolling memory buffer of about the last 30 seconds. That buffer exists so the app can play back the last thing you said after you pause (or when you tap **Replay what I said**). Playback is also handled on the device. The replay is limited to about the last 10 seconds of speech.

The app decides when you have paused by measuring sound level on the device. It does not send audio to a speech-recognition service, and it does not create a transcript.

Nothing in this path is written to disk, the Photos library, iCloud, or any other storage, and nothing is transmitted off the phone. The samples are discarded when the app process ends. Older audio also drops out of the buffer as new audio replaces it.

## Information stored on the device

The app stores two preferences in the app’s local settings, and only on this device:

- how long to wait before replaying (2–6 seconds; the default is 3)
- whether the first-run welcome screen has already been shown

These values are not personal data. They are not sent to the developer or to any server.

The app also reads the device uptime clock, only to ignore the microphone for a fraction of a second after a replay so it does not hear its own playback. That clock reading stays inside the app and is not recorded or transmitted.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, health data, or any other personal information.
- It does not collect device identifiers for tracking or advertising.
- It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software.
- It does not share data with third parties. The app makes no network requests for your content.
- The developer cannot listen to, download, or recover what the microphone heard.

The app’s privacy manifests declare no tracking and no collected data types.

## Microphone permission

iOS asks for microphone access after you tap **Continue** on the welcome screen. The system permission text states that the app listens only while it is open, so it can play back the last thing you said after a pause, and that audio stays on the device and is never uploaded or saved.

You can turn the microphone off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**. In Settings the app is listed as **Where Was I?**.

## Children

The app is a personal listening aid. It is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children.
