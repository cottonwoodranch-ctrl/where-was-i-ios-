## How the app works

Where Was I: Daily Recall helps you pick up a sentence when you lose your train of thought.

1. The first time you open the app, a short welcome screen explains what it does. Tap **Continue**. iOS then asks for the microphone.
2. While the app is open on the screen, it listens. On the screen the title is **Where Was I?**, and the status line says it listens only while the app is open.
3. Speak as you normally would. The app keeps about the last 30 seconds of sound in the phone’s memory. It does not save a recording and it does not send audio anywhere.
4. If you stop talking, the app waits, then plays the last thing you said (about the last 10 seconds) so you can continue. The wait is 3 seconds by default. The **Wait before replaying** slider sets it from 2 to 6 seconds.
5. **Replay what I said** plays that same bit again. Until the app has heard something it can still play, the button reads **Nothing to replay yet**.
6. **Pause listening** releases the microphone until you tap **Start listening**. Switching away from the app also stops listening. Coming back starts it again, unless you had paused it yourself.
7. Very short clicks and bumps (under about a third of a second) are ignored. A phone call, Siri, or another app using audio pauses listening until that audio finishes.

Playback uses the current route: the loudspeaker when nothing else is connected, or wired headphones, AirPods, or car audio when those are connected.

## Microphone permission

The app cannot listen until iOS microphone access is allowed.

**Allow it the first time.** After **Continue**, tap **Allow** on the system prompt.

**If you tapped Don’t Allow.** The app shows **The app needs the microphone**. Tap **Open Settings**. That opens this app’s page in Settings. Turn **Microphone** on, then return to the app. Listening starts again when the app is in the foreground.

You can also turn it on yourself:

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Privacy & Security → Microphone**.
3. Turn on **Where Was I?** (that is the name shown on the phone).
4. Open Where Was I: Daily Recall again.

**If it says “Could not start the microphone.”** Close the app completely and open it again. If another app is using the microphone, leave that app first.

**If it says “Paused while another app uses audio.”** Listening resumes when the call, Siri session, or other audio ends.

**If it says “Listening is paused.”** You tapped **Pause listening**. Tap **Start listening**.

**If nothing plays back.** Stay on the app’s screen, speak a normal sentence close to the phone, then stay quiet for the number of seconds on the slider. The orange microphone indicator in the status bar is on while the app is listening. **Replay what I said** stays disabled until there is something in memory to play.
